# Design principles

> The human provides the human things. The computer handles the computer things.

> I shouldn’t have to tell the computer to do computer things.

> Why is the human still the orchestration layer?

> A feature should reduce operation, not relocate it.

> Inference grants initiative, not unlimited authority.

> LLMs are components, not the architecture.

> Deterministic where possible; agentic where valuable.

> The interface is disposable; the model persists.

> Open-source the architecture of the world. Keep the lived world private.

These phrases constrain implementation. The system should retain durable state,
automate mechanical coordination, expose uncertainty, preserve provenance, and
ask the human only for judgment, consent, intent, or meaning. Convenience never
turns inferred context into permission.
